# Student

Sign in with your approved account. Registration waits for coordinator/administrator approval.

- **CBTP:** open Groups, submit needs assessment / action plan / reports. Returned work shows comments.
- **Internships:** browse opportunities, apply or propose a host, record attendance, fill weekly logbooks.
- **Project:** submit a title (similar titles are advisory scores, not a verdict), then update tasks and milestones.
- **FAQ:** English FAQ button. It cannot grade or approve work.
